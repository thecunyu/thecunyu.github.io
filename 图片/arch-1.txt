Weihai is a place with a long history and unique culture, where people once revered nature, drawing inspiration and strength from it to create any beautiful cultural landscapes, such as the terraced fields in the Rushan area. However, with the acceleration of modernization, people pursued economic benefits regardless of the balance of nature and ecological health, blasting and mining the local mountains and terraces extensively, causing severe environmental damage and pollution. This plundering and harm to nature has not only reduced the quality of life but also weakened the reverence and gratitude for nature, leading to the gradual decline of the original culture and arts rooted in nature.

The "Eco Echo" Art Museum in Weihai is a tribute to the profound dialogue between our cultural roots and the natural world. Drawing from the historical narrative of environmental degradation, our thematic exhibits – "Roots," "Rift," "Renewal," and "Return" – encapsulate the cycle of dissonance and harmony between human progress and ecological sanctity. "Roots" reflects our origins in nature's embrace, "Rift" portrays the discord of ecological exploitation, "Renewal" symbolizes the efforts toward environmental healing, and "Return" celebrates the potential for a harmonious coexistence. By invoking a sense of awe and reverence for nature through a phenomenological design approach, the museum not only showcases the region's artistic and natural beauty but also serves as a catalyst for ecological awareness and cultural revival in Weihai.
