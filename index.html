REC 01 Research 4 fields
I study how the built environment affects human well-being — combining text analysis, spatial data and deep learning to map mental health at the scale of cities. By treating millions of social-media posts as field notes, I try to uncover the systemic dynamics hidden in everyday human–environment interactions.

REC 03 Experience 4 records — tap to unfold
  • LooM indicator — a latent POI-sentiment measure of subjective emotion across 88 fine-grained urban place types.
  • NLP pipeline — processed a 2.78-million-post corpus; Llama-3.1 zero-shot filtering + fine-tuned Chinese RoBERTa sentiment classifier.
  • Spatial modelling — dual-stream deep architecture with cross-attention; urban scaling laws of depression risk across 302 cities.
  • CBEES framework — LDA + BERT embeddings extracting and weighting 48 spatial features from unstructured reviews.
  • Interpretable ML — Random Forest with SHAP & partial-dependence plots revealing non-linear threshold effects.
  • Longitudinal analysis — GAM time-series across 12 commercial projects, capturing post-COVID reopening dynamics.
  • Activity analysis — CV pipeline (YOLOv8, Faster R-CNN, DeepSORT) with BERT-based activity-duration extraction.
  • GNN simulation — graph architecture linking built-environment factors to individual restorative outcomes.
  • VR validation — spatial-scenario simulation with wearable biosensors (HRV, EDA) for physiological grounding.
  • Data strategy — systematic collection of geotagged text & imagery from Weibo and Dianping.
  • NLP × CV workflow — BERT sentiment classification with Faster R-CNN scene recognition.
  • Emotion recognition — Face++ facial-emotion API quantifying space–emotion correlations.
