Weihai is a place with a long history and unique culture, where peopleonce revered nature, drawing inspiration and strength from it to createmany beautiful cultural landscapes, such as the terraced fields in theRushan area. However, with the acceleration of modernization, peoplepursued economic benefits regardless of the balance of nature andecological health, blasting and mining the local mountains and terracesextensively, causing severe environmental damage and pollution. Thisplundering and harm to nature has not only reduced the quality of lifebut also weakened the reverence and gratitude for nature, leading tothe gradual decline of the original culture and arts rooted in nature.
